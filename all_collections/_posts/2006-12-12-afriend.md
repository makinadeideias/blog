title: What a friend is
date: 2003-12-14 10:00:00
categories: [poem]

What a friend is  
Needs not to be said
A friend is a mid-brother
from parents we never had

Open arms
When we fall
Helping hands
Support us all

What a friend is
Cannot be explained
On walking hard or even soft
Side by side, hand by hand

What a friend is
Cant be understood
Has no definition
Even though it should

What a friend is
Silly may mean
Though friendship
Is a god within

Having a friend
Is awesome one knows
Take care of yours
It helps us grow
